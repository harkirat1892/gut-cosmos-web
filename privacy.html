Privacy Policy — GUT COSMOS

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

GUT COSMOS ("the Game") is a single-player narrative exploration game created by Harkirat Singh for PC/Mac (Epic Games Store & Steam), iOS (Apple App Store), Android (Google Play & Epic Games Store Mobile), and Web.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

GUT COSMOS does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers. The Game operates 100% offline.

2. Local Save Data Only

Your game progress (current chapter, unlocked Codex entries, Milestones, and audio/display preferences) is stored exclusively on your own device using local browser/application storage (localStorage). You can erase all local save data at any time from inside the Game via Settings → Reset Data or by uninstalling the Game.

3. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

  • No analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK).
  • No third-party advertisements or tracking pixels.
  • No account creation or sign-in required.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Because GUT COSMOS collects no personal data whatsoever from any user of any age, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR provisions for minors.
